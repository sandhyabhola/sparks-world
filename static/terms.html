Terms and Privacy Policy

Who can use it: people 18 or older.

What we keep: your gamer name, language, answers, and designs you create. We do not ask for your real name. With Google sign-in we store only a scrambled ID, not your email or name. Using your real name is your choice.

Your email: we use it only to send a one-time code. We never read your inbox and our database never keeps the address itself, only a scrambled fingerprint that stops duplicate accounts and lets you reset a password. The email service that delivers the code may keep a copy of that message for a short time.

Your answers: personal details are removed automatically (no method is perfect). Answers are kept 30 days. Anonymous versions may appear in the idea forum and in public summaries. Something already published in an anonymous summary may not be removable.

Your ideas stay yours. You allow us to use them anonymously. Designs are shown only if you post them.

Rules: "Your gamer name is public on leaderboards. If you use your real name, anyone can see it. You can change it any time in Account Settings.", no people or characters in designs, nothing offensive to any religion, society or place, no bad words or insults. We may remove content or accounts that break the rules.

Your rights: delete your account any time from the Command Center. This removes your answers, designs, likes and comments.
